# Category, Vision, and Product

## Category Definition

Nakama's proposed long-term category is **autonomous mutuals**. Phase 0 is a
sponsor-funded protection program and should not be marketed as a legal mutual,
insurance policy, or member risk pool.

An autonomous mutual is a protection arrangement with five properties:

1. A defined group shares a bounded risk or support objective.
2. The terms, contribution rules, reserve state, and economic outcomes are
   explicit and independently verifiable.
3. Agents perform a material portion of design, administration, monitoring,
   evidence preparation, and reporting.
4. High-consequence decisions remain governed by published authority,
   challenge, and escalation rules.
5. Members do not surrender private medical or identity information to a
   public ledger.

"Autonomous" describes the operating system, not an absence of accountable
people or legal entities. "Mutual" describes shared protection and aligned
participation, not a claim that every deployment is legally incorporated or
regulated as a mutual insurer.

Public customer copy should normally use **community-owned, agent-operated
protection** until counsel confirms when the autonomous-mutual category term is
safe for a specific jurisdiction and structure.

## Vision

> Every trusted community can create a safety net for the risks its members
> actually face.

Today a community can form a treasury, issue credentials, coordinate work, run
events, and pay contributors globally. Creating a trustworthy protection system
still requires fragmented legal, actuarial, capital, claims, data, and software
infrastructure. Nakama makes that system configurable and reusable.

## Mission

Nakama's mission is to make protection portable, transparent, and community
governed while preserving the privacy and accountability that real-world harm
requires.

## Long-Term Product Surface

The mature network can contain four markets:

### Community demand

Community operators define groups, objectives, eligibility, contribution
methods, benefit rules, and governance. Members join through the community's
existing distribution rather than discovering protection through a generic
insurance marketplace.

### Agent operations

Specialist agents perform pool modelling, onboarding, reserve monitoring,
evidence completeness, fraud signals, member communications, care navigation,
reporting, and workflow coordination. Their performance becomes observable and
portable.

### Evaluation and dispute resolution

Qualified evaluators review cases within explicit scopes. Independent reviewers
can challenge or appeal decisions. Stakes, fees, service-level commitments, and
reputation create accountability, but clinical or legal qualifications remain
offchain credentials rather than token-holder votes.

### Capital and services

Sponsors, members, backstops, and future licensed capital partners fund bounded
exposure. Providers, assistance networks, payment partners, and other real-world
services fulfill approved outcomes. These roles enter only when their legal and
operational obligations are explicit.

## The Product Hierarchy

Nakama needs one consistent hierarchy:

| Layer | Name | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company/network | Nakama | Owns the category, protocol, safety standards, and ecosystem |
| Protocol | Nakama Protocol | Encodes pool, reserve, membership, claim, role, and settlement truth |
| Agent | Nakama Operator | Designs and operates pools within approved authority |
| First product | Genesis Protection Program | A sponsor-funded, bounded health-protection cohort for one community |
| Token | `$NAKAMA` | Coordinates and secures productive network participation |
| Reserve asset | Approved stablecoin | Funds obligations and payouts without token-volatility exposure |

Do not create separate master brands for the agent, mutual, protocol, and token.
The hierarchy should make Nakama easier to remember rather than forcing the
market to learn four unrelated stories.

## The First Product

The Genesis Protection Program is portable essential-health support for a
defined, globally mobile community. It is activated for one cohort only after
terms, sponsor budget, exposure limits, operations, and jurisdictions pass
their gates. Members do not contribute in Phase 0. This removes a legal and
behavioral variable while Nakama tests whether sponsors buy and members use the
program.

It should initially cover a short list of acute, unplanned events during a
defined travel or residency window. It should not attempt to provide universal
primary care, chronic-disease coverage, elective care, life insurance, income
replacement, and evacuation in the same first product.

The value proposition to the community operator is:

> Give members a credible health safety net with visible backing and one
> operating partner, without building a protection program from scratch.

The value proposition to a member is:

> Know what support exists, what backs it, what evidence is required, and how a
> decision will be made before an emergency happens.

## What Makes It Different

Nakama should win through the combined operating model:

- community-native distribution instead of broad retail acquisition
- cohort-specific terms instead of pretending one global policy fits all
- visible reserve and liability state instead of an opaque promise
- private evidence with public economic receipts
- agent-compressed administration with accountable approval
- reusable pool templates and reputation instead of bespoke consulting forever
- portable stablecoin settlement where legally and operationally appropriate

No single item is sufficient. A transparent reserve with bad claims operations
is a weak product. An excellent agent with no capital truth is a software demo.
A token with neither is speculation.

## Product Principles

### Protection before speculation

Member rights and reserves must remain insulated from token price, token-holder
governance swings, founder trading, and treasury runway.

### Explicit scope before broad claims

Every mutual names its cohort, dates, jurisdictions, eligible events,
exclusions, waiting periods, per-event limits, aggregate cap, reserve, review
authority, dispute process, and termination behavior.

### Private evidence, public economic truth

Raw evidence stays encrypted offchain. Public records contain the minimum
commitments needed to verify terms, authority, reserve effects, and settlement.

### Human accountability where harm is possible

Agents may automate low-risk administration and produce recommendations.
Adverse benefit decisions and material payouts require accountable authority
until evidence supports a narrower autonomous policy.

### One complete loop before a platform

The first objective is a real funded cohort, not a permissionless factory with
no operator, capital, or buyer.

### Progressive decentralization by function

Nakama should decentralize operator choice, verification, capital visibility,
template contribution, and dispute oversight at different rates. It should not
pretend that privacy, clinical responsibility, sanctions screening, or licensed
activities become safe because governance has more wallets.

## Expansion Logic

New products enter only after the current product meets its gate.

| Expansion | Required prior proof |
| --- | --- |
| A second health community | First community completes its window and renews, expands, or refers |
| A broader health benefit set | Claims data and capital support show the added exposure is affordable |
| A new geography | Counsel, operations, payments, and evidence handling are approved there |
| A new risk such as income or cyber | A qualified buyer, specialist operator, and bounded terms exist |
| Agent-performance protection | ACP usage creates measurable, recurring economic exposure |
| Permissionless pool creation | Templates, stake, monitoring, disputes, and emergency controls work under adversarial testing |
| External risk capital | Legal wrapper, disclosures, accounting, loss waterfall, and redemption logic are independently reviewed |

## Product Anti-Vision

Nakama is not building:

- a token whose primary utility is access to healthcare
- a claims DAO where anonymous holders inspect medical records
- a black-box AI insurer
- a global insurance promise without local authority
- a yield vault that quietly lends claims reserves
- a generic health chatbot with a token
- a marketplace that lists unreviewed protection pools beside legitimate ones
- a fundraising narrative that assumes ACF proceeds equal sustainable revenue

## The Ambition Test

The vision is sufficiently ambitious if one successful health mutual makes it
easier for the next community, agent, evaluator, service provider, and capital
partner to participate. It is empty ambition if each launch remains a founder-
operated exception whose only shared primitive is `$NAKAMA`.
